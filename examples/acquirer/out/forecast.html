Forecast against actual

Acme Payments, an invented merchant acquirer · October 2025 to 15 October 2026 · every figure is made up

The model made in May, fitted to the months through April, expected 3,114 active merchants and $1.22M of net revenue in September. September came in at 2,310 and $877k, 26 % and 28 % short. The miss is in new merchants: the model assumed 285 new merchants a month rising to 385, and Acme Payments added between 151 and 204.

Actual against the May forecast

Of the revenue gap in September, about 90 % is fewer merchants and the rest is lower revenue per merchant: $380 against the model's $392. Merchants were kept at 96.7 % to 97.3 % a month against the model's 97.5 %.
