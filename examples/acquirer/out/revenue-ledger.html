Revenue ledger

Acme Payments, an invented merchant acquirer · October 2025 to 15 October 2026 · every figure is made up

In September merchants paid Acme $1.78M in fees on $98.94M of card volume. $1.07M went on to card issuers as interchange and $147k to the card schemes, leaving a fee margin of $561k; other fees added $316k for net revenue of $877k. October has 15 of 31 days banked and is on pace for $915k.

Revenue on one scale

The three panels share one scale, $1M at the top, so the fee margin and the other fees read as parts of net revenue at their true size.

One bar per month from zero. October is drawn as what is banked (grey) and its pace for 31 days (dashed), never joined to the finished months.

The rates behind it

The take rate fell from 97 to 89 basis points. The merchant fee rate fell 15 points while interchange fell 3, so the fee margin went from 68 to 57 points of volume. The FX markup, on a cross-border share up from 16 % to 22 %, rose from 14 to 18 points, and instant payouts added 3, which made up part of the difference.
