September closed with $877k of net revenue on $98.94M of card volume from 2,310 active merchants. With 15 of 31 days banked, October is on pace for $915k, +4 % on September. Every measure the business watches is one row below.

Thirteen months, thirteen measures

Each row is one measure over thirteen months, October 2025 at the left. Totals are tiny bars from zero; ratios and counts of merchants are dots on the row's own range, so a row's shape compares with itself, not with its neighbours. The red mark is the latest: for totals October is the banked 15 days in grey with its 31-day pace as an open dashed bar, for ratios October's reading so far is a hollow dot. Active merchants are a headcount, a state read once a month, so they are dots too; October's month-to-date count does not scale with days and has no pace. Lost merchants are counted when a month closes, from November 2025. Changes compare September, the last whole month.
