What drives revenue

Acme Payments, an invented merchant acquirer · October 2025 to 15 October 2026 · every figure is made up

Net revenue is active merchants times net revenue per active merchant. In September that was 2,310 × $380 = $877k. Since October 2025 the merchant count grew 86 % and revenue per merchant 2 %, so nearly all of the growth is merchants: how many start taking payments, and how many stay.

The three drivers

Who stays

Small merchants are almost half the count and a fourteenth of the volume; they also leave fastest. A merchant kept 95.2 % a month stays about 21 months; one kept 99.5 % stays about 200 months.

What a signed merchant is worth, September

September's channels signed 356 merchants, of whom 204 became active: the 204 new merchants above. Value per signed merchant is its first twelve months of contribution: the share activated × $380 (September's net revenue per active merchant) × 40 % contribution margin × 10.2, the expected months of the first twelve a merchant stays when 97.1 % are kept each month. Platform plug-ins return the most per dollar (4.6), referrals next (4.5); direct sales earns less in its first twelve months than it costs to sign (0.8).
