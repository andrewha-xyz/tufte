Of every $100 a merchant ran through Acme Payments in September, the merchant paid $1.80; $1.24 went on to issuers and card schemes, $0.89 stayed as net revenue and $0.35 was left after costs. Contribution margin rose from 16 % in October 2025 to 40 % because the platform, 78 % of September's costs, grows slower than volume.
