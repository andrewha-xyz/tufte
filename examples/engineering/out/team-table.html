The team in one table

Acme Payments engineering, an invented team of 22 · 13 April to 15 October 2026 · every figure is made up

Twenty-two engineers merged 2,316 pull requests between April 13 and October 15 and gave 3,060 reviews. Within each team the busiest reviewer gave more than an even share, from 22 % of their team's reviews (Mike, Merchant dashboard) to 33 % (Golf, Risk and disputes). After Kilo left on Jul 6, Risk and disputes' median wait for a first review went from 2.9 h in June to 8.1 h in September.

How to read it

The table shows how pull requests and review load are spread across people. A pattern such as one person giving most of a team's reviews, or a team's wait for a first review growing after someone leaves, is a prompt for a conversation with that team, not a ranking. Counts of pull requests are not a measure of performance, and the columns are deliberately not sortable or scored.
