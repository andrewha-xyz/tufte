Pull request flow

Acme Payments engineering, an invented team of 22 · 13 April to 15 October 2026 · every figure is made up

In September a merged pull request spent a median 9.9 h being written before it was opened, 3.6 h waiting for its first review, 4.0 h in review and 1.4 h between merge and production. Risk and disputes lost a reviewer on Jul 6: its median wait for a first review went from 2.9 h in June to 8.1 h in September, while the other three teams waited 3.4 h. Of 3,060 reviews, Charlie gave 226 and Victor 196; the median engineer gave 154.

Where the time goes

A median is a single number per stage, so each is one bar from zero on one scale for every team; the value is printed at the end of the bar instead of on an axis. Each stage has its own median, so the four do not add up to the median pull request.

Waiting for a first review, by week

A week's waits are a distribution, so each week is a quartile plot: the line runs from the first to the third quartile of the pull requests merged that week, broken around the median dot. One scale for all four teams; the week of Oct 12 has 4 of 5 working days and is drawn hollow.

Pull request size

One quartile plot per team, Tufte's redesign of the box plot: grey lines from the smallest pull request to the first quartile and from the third quartile to the largest, the middle half in black, offset, broken by the median dot. Size spans three orders of magnitude, so the scale is logarithmic. The share over 400 lines is a ratio, so it is a dot.

Review load

One dot per engineer: pull requests authored across, reviews given up. Names are printed wherever they fit; hover any dot for the rest.
