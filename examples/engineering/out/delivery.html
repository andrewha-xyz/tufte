Deploys a week

Lead time, first commit to production

Each week is a quartile plot: the line runs from the first to the third quartile of that week's changes, broken around the median dot. The scale is logarithmic so an hour and a week both read; the current week is hollow.

Failures and restores

A change failure rate is a ratio, so one dot a month; hover a dot for the deploys behind it, because a month with few deploys swings hard. 3.9 % of all 1,929 deploys failed.
